### [Glassbox](https://seeglassbox.com)
Job ads list everything. Glassbox counts what today's jobs really ask for, and shows you where you stand.

- A daily pool of public jobs, each counted once
- A fixed skill vocabulary, so a phrase never counts as a skill
- Your CV is read for skills; the file is never kept

Next.js · FastAPI · Postgres · Azure · Vercel

## Rebuilt, honestly

Two projects where the first version looked good and was wrong. Finding out why was the real work.

| | The first version | What I rebuilt |
|---|---|---|
| [**XGauges**](https://xgauges.vercel.app) · [code](https://github.com/DerrickWawerumuturi/xgauges) | A Random Forest with strong metrics. Its label was arithmetic on its own features: it was recovering a subtraction, not learning football. | A transparent Euro 2024 index, kept apart from a real forecast of 2025/26 Premier League output, cross validated against baselines. The honest result: nothing beat a minutes only baseline (ρ 0.54), and I published that. |
| [**SongGrader**](https://sgd-eight.vercel.app) · [code](https://github.com/DerrickWawerumuturi/sgd) | Claimed to classify songs as positive or negative. It compared lyrics to sentences about lyrics with mean pooled BERT, and got it wrong most of the time. | Explains what a song is about, and every interpretation quotes the lyrics. Every quote is checked against the source automatically. |
